Review 7 accepted M1.3 as an exploratory milestone and kept confirmatory zero-information testing blocked. It authorised one bounded repair of the CRT sampler and the start of M2A (engineering pilot design). This note records where the review's instructions are underspecified or could be satisfied in a way that defeats their purpose. It proposes how to resolve each point before any design is frozen. Nothing here has been executed.

- The four CPER gains (+0.020, +0.022, −0.025, +0.073 nats/row) stay descriptive, with all six sensitivities retained.

## Points to settle

### 1 · A repaired generator makes its own calibration targets uninformative

The review prefers a simulation-calibrated correction for the within-day AR. Such a calibration chooses the AR coefficient so that the simulated day-demeaned lag-1 statistic matches the observed one, which is the right estimator. But then F1's within-day lag-1 check passes by construction. The same holds for the hour/temperature scale model (F3's sd_hour) and for resampled innovations (F4's shape). An audit that reuses the fitted statistics cannot detect an inadequate sampler.

**Proposal.** Declare two disjoint sets before execution.
- *Calibration targets:* within-day lag 1, day-level lag 1, scale by hour and temperature tercile, and the marginal innovation distribution.
- *Held-out diagnostics:*
  - within-day lags 2–3;
  - lag-1 autocorrelation of squared residuals (volatility clustering);
  - day-level lag 7;
  - lagged cross-feature correlations;
  - joint tail exceedance across features;
  - temperature dependence of the scale within hour.

For the real-feature check, fit on alternate weeks within each season and diagnose on the other weeks. Leave-one-season-out fit would be reported only descriptively. The CRT sampler is fitted per stratum on the analysed data and never has to extrapolate across seasons, so a cross-season miss (2022 phenology) would not bear on its validity.

### 2 · Synthetic worlds must lie outside the sampler's model class

New worlds built from an AR process with a scale model and resampled innovations would be passed trivially by the repaired sampler. Notebook 16 failed in exactly this way: its worlds were Gaussian and homoscedastic like its sampler, so the within-day defect went unseen.

**Proposal.**
- **Null worlds**, generated by mechanisms the sampler does not contain:
  - (i) real CPER V with synthetic labels (see §3);
  - (ii) Markov regime switching between clear, cloudy and convective day types;
  - (iii) GARCH-type volatility clustering;
  - (iv) a long-memory day-level process.
- **Deliberately misspecified samplers:**
  - (a) the current sampler;
  - (b) within-day AR set to 0;
  - (c) Gaussian and homoscedastic;
  - (d) features drawn independently, which breaks F2;
  - (e) persistence inflated by 50 %.
- **The audit's own validation:** the audit counts as validated only if its verdict separates the samplers whose Type I exceeds 0.10 in these worlds from those whose Type I does not.

### 3 · The semi-synthetic null should become the primary validity evidence, with many independent labels

What the CRT needs is Type I conditional on the observed images V, because that is the only V it will be applied to. The old F5 recombined four years of real label residuals into 39 datasets. Those datasets are few and mutually dependent. At 39, a single rejection is compatible with a true rate up to about 0.12 (one-sided 95 %).

**Proposal.**
- Generate D* = M(C) + ε* with ε* from a label-only residual generator, for example day-means from an AR process with resampled within-day blocks. The generator is fitted to the label residuals given C, without V.
- Draw at least 200 datasets, conditionally independent given V. That bounds the rate to about ±0.03.
- Audit the residual generator against the real label residuals' persistence. This is a label-only check; it never relates labels to images.
- The persistence of D* matters: F5 detects an under-persistent sampler only if D* is as persistent as the real D, which is why F1 had to be decisive in notebook 17.

**Requested decision.** For the *new* sampler, with this declared before execution, should the primary criterion be this conditional F5 and the §1 held-out diagnostics be secondary? This changes no past verdict.

### 4 · Power sets the scientific value of a valid CPER test

At the injected ≈ 0.03 nats, the CRT detected 0.28 and the shift test 0.44 (notebook 17). The observed gains are about 0.02. Even a valid sampler would leave an exploratory Q0 on 2020–2023 underpowered for effects of the size seen.

**Proposal.**
- The experiment reports the minimum detectable effect at 80 % power on CPER.
- It is declared now that if that effect exceeds about 0.05 nats, the sampler's value is as a reusable method for field-pilot data, not as a NEON result.

**Requested decision.** If the repaired sampler passes, is an exploratory Q0 on 2020–2023 authorised, or is the pass only a methods result?

### 5 · "Fails materially" needs a definition before execution

**Proposal.** NEON Q0 development closes for this phase if any of these holds:
1. The Type I upper 95 % bound exceeds 0.10 in any null world, including the semi-synthetic ones on real V.
2. The audit misclassifies a misspecified sampler whose Type I exceeds 0.10.
3. The held-out diagnostics fail at their declared threshold, the same 0.10 share used in notebook 17.
4. The budget is exceeded: one declared design, one synthetic-validation notebook and one real re-audit notebook, with no second design iteration.

### 6 · Resampling residual days assumes they are exchangeable given C

Standardised residual-day resampling preserves cross-feature dependence, within-day dependence and tails. But it is valid only if the within-day residual pattern does not depend on C. Cloudiness shapes the within-day pattern of the image features and correlates with temperature, which is in C.

**Proposal.**
- Resample whole days within strata of day-level context, for example the tercile of daily maximum temperature within season.
- With about 30 days per stratum, the null draws become near-permutations of real days. The experiment reports how discrete the CRT null distribution is.
- The fallback is a parametric skewed-t innovation with a Gaussian copula.

### 7 · The field pilot should be designed around named physical pathways

The pathway determines the site-admission criteria, the regions of interest, the frame schedule and the reference sensors.

- **P1, hygroscopic aerosol growth.** Path extinction rises with RH. This is the most physically grounded pathway.
  - It needs dark targets at several known ranges (roughly 0.5–10 km) for contrast transmittance.
  - It needs a dry-aerosol measurement, because aerosol loading is a confounder already named in the E2 framing, and review 7's hardware list omits one. Low-cost optical PM sensors are themselves RH-sensitive, so this needs a heated or dried inlet, or an explicit limitation.
- **P2, dew and surface wetness near saturation.** Near-field targets. It needs a leaf-wetness sensor as a QC and stratification variable, not in C.
- **P3, vegetation water status.** Slow, and confounded with season, as CPER 2022 showed. It is the least identifiable at a single site.

**Proposals for the minimum tier.**
- *In-scene reference panels* (grey, black and white, both sunlit and shaded) and a *diffuse/direct radiation split*.
- With RAW frames, the panels convert digital numbers to a reflectance-like quantity per frame, which separates scene change from illumination change.
- That is the main defence against the illumination pathway that fixed exposure leaves untouched.

**Requested decision.** Which pathway or pathways should the pilot be powered for?

### 8 · NEON's remaining role

NEON is better kept as an external replication site for a frozen estimator developed on field data (CPER 2024–2025 reserved) than used as a development site. The decision analysis will argue this against continued NEON statistical work.
