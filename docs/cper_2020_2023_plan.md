# CPER 2020–2023: frozen acquisition, QC and analysis plan

**Status:** frozen before any bulk image download for 2020–2022 and before any visual feature is computed for those years (review 5, item 6). Any change after this point is a recorded deviation (§9). The incremental audit that follows this plan is [notebook 15](../notebooks/15_cper_2020_2023_acquisition_audit.ipynb).

## 1 · Why CPER 2020–2023

- Notebook 13 found one camera unit throughout (MAC 0030F4D0A44B), an identical configuration in every sampled frame, and a consistent view: edge correlation 0.66–0.84 against 2023, with no shift.
- Sensor positions are unchanged, and the July label is ≥ 98 % valid in each of these years.
- CPER 2019 is excluded: its view differs (44 px, with a different horizon and scale) and its July label is 58 % valid.

## 2 · Prior exposure of each year

| summer | what has already been looked at | status |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | labels, hourly images, the nine features, and the declared visual contrast with its diagnostics (notebooks 07–12) | **informed methodological choices**; not untouched |
| 2020–2022 | July tower data and one midday frame per week (camera audit, notebook 13); no feature computed, no image related to a label | exploratory, unanalysed |

## 3 · Camera epoch and frame QC

- **Epoch.** 2020-06-01 to 2023-08-31 is one epoch. Each season's midday frames are checked against the 2023 reference: phase-correlation shift ≤ 8 px and edge correlation ≥ 0.5. A season that fails becomes its own epoch and stratum, and its regions are re-checked before use.
- **Frames.** Hourly frames on the hour, 06–19 LST; the `.meta` must be present, the overlay clock must agree with the file-name time to ≤ 60 s, and the configuration must equal the epoch reference (auto-haze, AGC, auto-exposure, white balance, resolution, IR off, overlay position). Frames failing any of these are excluded and counted.
- **Rows.** A row is usable only if the frame passes, the 1-min label record at its UTC minute is valid, and incoming shortwave exceeds 10 W m⁻².

## 4 · Image regions and features

- **Regions.** `features.REGIONS['CPER']` (sky, far and near bands) and the overlay mask are frozen as of notebook 09; they are not re-tuned per season.
- **Features.** Only the nine predefined features (D-049).
- **Sky rule.** The sky features are dropped for the whole block if the median clipped-sky share exceeds 0.2 in *any* season, so the feature set is identical in every fold.
- **Acquisition state.** Exposure enters only the acquisition-only controls (`e2_acq_control`, `e2_illum_acq_control`).

## 5 · Labels

- The D-046 measurement model: label `D_h = T_asp − Td`, with `D_probe` as a mandatory sensitivity.
- Minutes with D_h < −2σ(D_h) are excluded as inconsistent.
- RH_implied ≥ 98.3 % is a sensitivity stratum, never censored.
- Every sensitivity is reported both as frozen-model deletion and as a full refit (D-066).

## 6 · Context, estimands and splits

- **Estimands.** E2 is primary. E2_illum is secondary, with K = SW / GHI_clear (Haurwitz clear-sky model, solar elevation > 10°, K clipped to [0, 1.5]).
- **Context.** Context and capacity follow whichever protocol passes the notebook 14 validation. If none passes, no Q0 claim is made, and only descriptive season-specific G is reported.
- **Splits.** Leave one year out over 2020–2023 (4 folds). The chronological fit on 2020–2022 with evaluation on 2023 is reported descriptively, and always labelled as evaluated on a year that informed the method.
- **Strata.** Seasons, and camera epochs if any are found in §3.
- **Reserved years.** 2024–2025 are not used for anything in this block.

## 7 · Download plan (incremental)

1. Tower-top tables for every June–August month of 2020–2023 (about 23 MB per site-month), then label QC. *Notebook 15.*
2. Hourly frame listings and `.meta` files for 2020–2022, then frame QC (§3). *Notebook 15.*
3. Hourly JPEGs for 2020–2022 (about 1.2 GB), with a SHA-256 manifest. *After this plan, and only once the inference protocol has passed validation.*
4. Feature extraction: the §4 rules, with no label involved. *After step 3.*

## 8 · What is not done in this block

- No new feature or region is tuned on any year.
- No frozen encoder or large visual backbone is trained.
- No HARV year is pooled: its camera epochs, its July 2021 label fault and its 2022 radiation anomaly are unresolved (D-065).
